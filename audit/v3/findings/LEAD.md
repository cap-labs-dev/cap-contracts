### [MEDIUM] A market owner can swap the collateral basis after GOVERNOR sized the credit line, and withdraw the original collateral while the debt is outstanding
**Location:** contracts/cap/Registry.sol:L177-L199 (`createTranche`), contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol:L270-L289 (`lockedValue`), L307-L322 (`creditLimit`, `variableCreditLimit`)
**Impact:** `fixedCreditLimit` (GOVERNOR) is the only throttle on credit creation and it is a per-market notional, not a per-asset one. Once set, the owner appends a junior tranche in *any* asset the oracle prices, funds it, and every senior's `lockedValue` falls to zero because the junior now carries `debt/(lt−buffer)`. The owner then `instantRedeem`s the senior. The cUSD holders' exposure moves from the asset GOVERNOR evaluated to one it did not, with no governance action and no event that says so. In the PoC a 500k cUSD line sized against WETH ends up backed only by ALT.
**Likelihood:** Owner role only (any WHITELISTED address mints one), plus one other asset with a price source. No cost beyond gas and posting the substitute collateral. Also happens without malice: it is how the product lets underwriters rotate out.
**Exploit path:** (1) eve (WHITELISTED) → `createChildRoles` → `createFloatingMarket([WETH],[1e27],…)`; sets ltv 0.7, borrower role = self, deposits 1,000,000 WETH-units. (2) GOVERNOR `setFixedCreditLimit(500_000e18)`. (3) eve borrows 500,000 cUSD; health 1.6. (4) eve `createTranche(market, ALT, [0.5,0.5])`, grants herself the depositor role, deposits 1,000,000 ALT. (5) senior `unlockedSupply() == totalSupply()`; eve `instantRedeem`s all WETH; `totalCapital` is now 1,000,000 ALT, health ≥ 1. Net: eve holds 500k cUSD, all of her WETH, and cUSD holders are exposed to ALT.
**Proof:** FAILING on HEAD: `Expected.t.sol::Expected_P14::test_EXPECTED_seniorStaysLockedWhenOwnerAppendsJunior` — `[FAIL: senior collateral must stay locked: 1000000000000000000000000 >= 1000000000000000000000000]`. Demonstration: `P14_Whitelisted.t.sol::test_P14_ownerSwapsCollateralBasisAfterSizing` (PASS = behaviour present). Companion: `test_P14_noCreditUntilGovernorSetsLimit` proves `fixedCreditLimit` defaults to 0 and is the sole gate; `test_P14_selfUnderwrittenLineAfterGovernorLimit` proves a single party can be owner, sole depositor and sole borrower.
**Recommendation:** Make the credit limit per collateral asset (or cap each tranche's contribution to `variableCreditLimit`), and require GOVERNOR (not the owner) to approve the asset list of a market at creation and on `createTranche`. Second-order: underwriter rotation needs an explicit governance step.
**Invariant broken:** I27 in spirit (the set of assets backing outstanding debt is not invariant under owner action).

### [MEDIUM] A fixed draw is charged the catch-up on *floating* notional that already pays the higher rate through its own index, and a same-block floating borrow+repay sandwich raises a victim's fixed premium at zero cost
**Location:** contracts/cap/market/FixedMarket.sol:L337-L351 (`_borrowPremium`), L186-L208 (`availableCredit`); contracts/cap/InterestRateModel.sol:L220-L224 (`unsmoothedCredit`), L227-L239; contracts/cap/market/FloatingMarket.sol:L170-L171 (`_chargePremium` same-timestamp early return)
**Impact:** `_borrowPremium` prices `prior = unsmoothedCredit()` (global, every market) at the post-mint rate and charges the new fixed borrower the difference. When `prior` is *fixed* credit that is correct (it paid rate₀ upfront and never reprices). When `prior` is *floating* credit it is a double charge: the floating loan's index already accrues at the new utilization. Measured: with 500k floating drawn in the window, a 500k/30-day fixed draw pays 11,472.60 vs 11,130.14 once the same 500k is absorbed into the average (+342.47, +3.1%). Adversarially: a borrower-role holder draws 2M floating, the victim draws 500k fixed, the attacker repays in the same block — the attacker's premium is 0 (`_chargePremium` returns early on `lastPremiumUpdate == block.timestamp`; repaid 1,999,999.999…) and the victim's premium is 12,271.69 instead of 10,787.67 (+13.8%). The surplus goes to stcUSD stakers and tranches, so an attacker who stakes recovers part of it.
**Likelihood:** Honest case: every fixed draw in a window with outstanding floating draws overpays by `C·term·(rate_P − rate_0)`. Adversarial case: needs a borrower role and an unused floating line ≥ C; cost is gas only.
**Exploit path:** see `test_P6_sameBlockSandwichCostsAttackerNothing`: (1) attacker `fl.borrow(2_000_000e18)`; (2) victim `fx.borrow(500_000e18, 30 days)` → debt = 512,271.69; (3) attacker `fl.repay(max)` → 1,999,999.999… burned, premium 0. Victim overpaid 1,484.02 cUSD.
**Proof:** FAILING on HEAD: `Expected.t.sol::Expected_P6::test_EXPECTED_fixedPremiumIndependentOfSameBlockFloatingNotional` — `[FAIL: a transient floating draw must not reprice a fixed draw: 12271689497716894977169 != 10787671232876712328766]`. Demonstration: `P6_FixedCrossNotional.t.sol` (both PASS = behaviour present).
**Recommendation:** Track unsmoothed *fixed* credit separately from floating credit in the IRM (only fixed draws need catch-up), and charge floating markets premium on the same-block path (drop the early return, or compute premium for `elapsed == 0` at the post-mint rate). Second-order: the EMA becomes two-sided; `availableCredit(term)`'s catch-up deduction must use the fixed-only figure too.
**Invariant broken:** none listed; I32's "premium fits inside the limit" still holds. New: "a borrower's premium depends only on its own notional and the market state, not on same-window notional of other markets that reprice on their own".

### [LOW] Credit-backed cUSD earns the liquidity premium: a borrower that opts in is paid a share of the yield it is paying
**Location:** contracts/utils/PremiumVesting.sol:L114-L123 (`optIn`), L200-L218 (`_update`); contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:L83-L86 (`fundCreditBacked`), L309-L314 (`_mintCreditBacked`)
**Impact:** The liquidity premium is minted to the stablecoin pot and vested pro rata to *opted-in balances*, with no distinction between reserve-backed and credit-backed cUSD. A borrower that keeps its borrowed cUSD and calls `optIn` captures `D/(D+staked)` of the pot. Measured at 50% utilization with one 1M lender and one 1M borrower over a year: liquidity pot ≈ 84,639.57; borrower claimed 42,319.79, lender 42,319.79; the borrower's 324,706.12 of total premium fell to an effective 282,386.33 (−13%). stcUSD yield is diluted one-for-one. The cost is always positive, so it is a leak, not an exploit.
**Likelihood:** One call, no capital beyond the loan. Applies to any holder of borrowed cUSD, including tranches (which receive credit-backed cUSD as premium and could opt in on the stablecoin).
**Exploit path:** `test_P8_borrowerCapturesLiquidityPremium`.
**Proof:** FAILING on HEAD: `Expected.t.sol::Expected_P8::test_EXPECTED_borrowerEarnsNoLiquidityPremium` — `[FAIL: credit-backed balance must not earn the liquidity premium: 42319786527986081874639 != 0]`. Demonstration: `P8_BorrowerOptIn.t.sol` (PASS = behaviour present).
**Recommendation:** Pay liquidity premium only on reserve-backed supply: e.g. track `optIn` eligibility per account minus its outstanding credit-backed mints, or route the pot to `Wrapper` only. Second-order: the Wrapper is then the sole beneficiary, which the deploy already assumes.
**Invariant broken:** none.

## Trust-model observation (for the report, not a ranked finding)
`fixedCreditLimit` defaults to 0, so a WHITELISTED address cannot mint credit without GOVERNOR sizing its market. Everything else in the credit lifecycle — owner, depositor allowlist (settable to PUBLIC_ROLE, `test_P14_depositorRoleCanBePublic`), borrower set, ltv up to lt−buffer, collateral composition, underwriter rate — sits with the market owner. The protocol's coverage promise therefore reduces to: GOVERNOR sizes a notional per market, and the owner decides what backs it.

### [MEDIUM] A market owner can set a funded, locked junior tranche's premium weight to zero while it remains first-loss (routed from WS-R incidental #2)
**Location:** contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol:L119-L127 (`setTrancheWeights`), L390-L406 (`_setTranches` — only checks Σ weights == 1e27, no per-tranche floor), L435-L475 (`_chargePremium` pays `underwriterPremium.rayMul(weight)`), L367-L373 (`_liquidate` slashes juniors first regardless of weight)
**Impact:** Third-party depositors in a junior tranche (e.g. an Underwriter vault allocated there) keep carrying first-loss exposure and stay locked by `lockedValue`, but receive no underwriter premium; the owner redirects their share to the senior (which the owner may own) or, if the senior is not opted in, to cUSD stakers. Weight is the only compensation lever and it is unilateral.
**Likelihood:** Owner role only; one call; no capital. Depositors cannot exit while locked.
**Exploit path:** (1) owner creates market [senior: own capital, junior: third-party underwriter]; junior locked by outstanding debt. (2) owner `setTrancheWeights([1e27, 0])`. (3) `chargePremium` → 100% to senior; junior earns 0; a liquidation still slashes the junior first.
**Proof:** to be produced by the verifier (see verify/LEAD-3.md); REGRESSION.md incidental #2 describes the observation.
**Recommendation:** Require a minimum weight for any tranche with `totalCapital > 0` while the market has debt, or make weight changes on indebted markets a GOVERNOR co-signed action; emit the change with old/new weights.
**Invariant broken:** proposed I43: "no tranche with locked capital has weight 0".
