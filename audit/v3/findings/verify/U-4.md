# U-4 verification — migrated token reports full backing while the reserve is off-contract and "unreachable by any new function"

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED-AT-LOWER-SEVERITY (Low as a HEAD code finding; its substance is a migration-plan requirement that belongs in the merged U-1 checklist). The numbers are right; "unrecoverable" / "permanently stranded" is wrong under the finding's own precondition; "reports full backing while not on hand" is HEAD's documented and tested design, not a migration artefact.

## Numbers verified (publicnode, block 25,977,255)

All of the author's figures reproduce: `USDC.balanceOf(cUSD)` 3,219.190527; `totalSupply` 84,884,291.50; v1 `totalSupplies(USDC)` 79,759,278.00; `totalBorrows(USDC)` 61,486,536.08; `loaned(USDC)` 18,269,522.95 = FR `maxWithdraw(cUSD)`; `totalSupplies(wWTGXX)` 5,124,039.28; `paused()==false`. Two facts the author did not record and that matter for "unreachable": the USDC FR vault `0x3Ed6aa…` is an EIP-1167 clone (target `0xd806…`, 47 KB) whose `totalSupply()` equals `balanceOf(cUSD)` (17,523,486.66 shares — cUSD is the sole depositor); and the v1 Lender `0x15622c…` is an ERC1967 proxy (impl `0x68c4…`, 329-byte code) under the same v1 AccessControl/timelock as cUSD, i.e. upgradeable.

## What I checked

`Stablecoin.totalAssets()` = `backing()` scaled (`contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:200-202`), `backing()` = `totalSupply − badDebt`. `unlockedSupply()` = `min(supply − credit − badDebt, quoteWithdraw(USDC.balanceOf(this)))` (`:184-192`) and every redemption path — `instantRedeem` via `maxInstantRedeem`, the queue via `_claimableShares`/`_claimFifo` (`contracts/ERC7540/ERC7540AsyncRedeem.sol:337-358, 372-408`) — is capped by it. `invest`/`recall` speak only `IAeraVault.deposit/withdraw(TokenAmount[])` (`:102-117`); there is no ERC-4626 passthrough and no rescue. Post-migration with a correct reinitializer: `totalAssets()==84,884,291.50 USDC`, `unlockedSupply()==3,219.19 cUSD` (my `test_b_reported…`). All as the author says.

## Strongest attacks and where they land

**(4) "The system reports itself covered while depositors cannot redeem" is HEAD's design, not a defect introduced by migration.** `IStablecoin.sol:154-158` documents `totalAssets` as "recognized backing … Integrators read this as managed assets", and `test/unit/cap/Stablecoin.t.sol:921 test_invest_movesReserveAndLeavesSharePrice` asserts `totalAssets()==1,000` with 600 on hand — literally "accounting is not the token balance". The author's `test_FAIL_5` predicate (`totalAssets() <= USDC.balanceOf(cUSD)`) fails on a *fresh* HEAD deployment after any `invest()` (my `test_b_reported…` shows 1,000 > 600 with `unlockedSupply()==600`). `unlockedSupply` is the honest figure and it caps every exit, so the token never pays out what it does not hold. What is migration-specific is only that the off-contract reserve sits in v1 positions instead of Aera.

**(2) "cannot be reached by any new function" — partly true, but not "unrecoverable" / "permanently stranded".**
- *61.49 M USDC on loan to v1 agents.* HEAD needs no entry point to receive it: a plain USDC transfer to the proxy raises `unlockedSupply` 1:1 (`test_b_plainUsdcTransfer…`: +61,486e18 for +61,486e6). What breaks is the *Lender-side* bookkeeping (`Lender.repay → IVault(cUSD).repay` selector gone), and the Lender is itself an upgradeable v1 proxy under the same timelock, so repayment/liquidation can be re-routed to a direct transfer. Outside HEAD, but not stranded. The genuine gap: HEAD has no state for "reserve on loan to v1 agents" — after migration `creditBackedSupply==0`, so `utilizationRate()==0` while 61.5 M is out and the IRM gets the wrong signal until repaid (asserted in the same test).
- *18.27 M USDC of FR shares and 5.12 M wWTGXX.* HEAD indeed has no path: `recall` reverts with `reserveVault==0` and with the ERC-4626 vault as target (no `withdraw((address,uint256)[])`), confirmed. But the finding's precondition is "a correct reinitializer", i.e. `authority()` is set, and then the proxy is upgradeable — the author's own `test_TABLE_5` log says so ("… after granting ADMIN to the timelock ok? true"). A one-off follow-up implementation (`redeemV1Vault`, `sweep`, both `restricted`) redeems the FR shares to the proxy and sweeps the second asset, then steps back to HEAD; USDC on hand goes 3,219 → 21,488 and `instantRedeem` pays par (`test_b_leftoverV1Positions…`). So "any residual at execution time is permanently stranded" is false; a residual costs one more 1-day timelock upgrade. (And it is avoidable up-front by v1 `divestAll` for both assets, which the author lists.)
- *wWTGXX re-denominated in USDC that is not there.* Correct as a wind-down item: ~5.12 M of the 84.88 M backing is not USDC and HEAD is single-asset, so it must be converted, redeemed through v1 `burn`/`redeem`, or recorded as `badDebt`. Keep this line; it is the one piece of the finding that is not implied by "migrate the reserve".

**(3)/(4) "New deposits become other people's exit liquidity".** Reproduced (alice deposits 1,000; bob's `requestRedeem(4,000)` is fully claimable; alice's `instantRedeem` reverts). But this is par in / par out through a FIFO queue — the same behaviour HEAD has whenever the reserve is invested — and alice completes at par as soon as a repayment lands (`test_c_…`). Timing risk, no value loss (badDebt 0, no discount). The framing "exit liquidity"/"lender of last resort" overstates it; the correct framing is "redemptions are FIFO against on-hand until the v1 reserve is brought over".

**(1) Precondition.** The scenario requires the batch to execute at today's state without a wind-down. Nothing in the repo scripts or enforces one (confirmed: `script/` is dead v1 code), so "conditional on migration sequencing" is fair — but it is an operations finding about a plan that does not exist yet, not a code path a third party can trigger.

## Recommendation to the lead

**Demote to Low and re-word; move the substance into U-1's migration checklist.** Strike "unrecoverable through the new code", "permanently stranded", "no contractual repayment path" and the exit-liquidity framing; strike the `test_FAIL_5` predicate (it is not a HEAD invariant — `test/unit/cap/Stablecoin.t.sol:921` asserts the opposite). Keep, as migration requirements: (i) HEAD cannot represent v1 loans or a multi-asset basket, so before the batch: repay/liquidate v1 debt, `divestAll` both assets, convert or burn the wWTGXX-backed share of supply (or record the shortfall as `badDebt`), and prove `USDC.balanceOf(proxy)·1e12 ≥ totalSupply()` on a fork; (ii) until that holds, `utilizationRate()` reads 0 with 61.5 M out and redemptions are FIFO against on-hand only; (iii) plan for a follow-up upgrade to recover any residual v1 position (or add the author's guarded reinitializer that reverts on shortfall). I would not adopt the permanent `rescue(token,to)` sweep in HEAD — the follow-up-upgrade path already exists behind the timelock and leaves no standing trusted power. The wWTGXX line is the one item here that is not implied by U-1 and deserves its own bullet.
