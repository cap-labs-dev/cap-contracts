### [Medium] G-1 — Liquidity premium is paid only to opted-in cUSD, so a borrower that parks its loan opted-in earns more than it pays whenever staked/S < u/(1 + uw/r) (P8)
**Location:** contracts/utils/PremiumVesting.sol:L114-123 (`optIn`, public, no restriction beyond self/DeadShares); contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:L83-86 (`fundCreditBacked`); contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol:L438-441 (`_chargePremium` liquidity leg)
**Impact:** Yield to a staked cUSD is `r(u)·u/φ_T` (φ_T = opted-in share of total supply) while a borrower's all-in cost is `r(u) + uw`. Whenever φ_T < u/(1+uw/r) the borrow-and-park loop is positive carry with zero capital at risk on the borrower's side (the loan is unsecured from the borrower; tranche underwriters carry it). Each loop mints credit-backed cUSD with no reserve behind it, consumes underwriter-backed credit for a circular position, and dilutes genuine stakers down to the borrower rate. At u = 0.8, harness rates (r = 10.48% after mint, uw 20%), staked $20M of $100M: a $5M parked loan nets +5.14% of D = **$257k/yr**; at u = 0.9 with the same opt-in, +61.27% of D. The loop self-limits only when staked/S reaches φ* = 26.7% (u 0.8, uw 20%), 53.3% (uw 5%), 80% (uw 0).
**Likelihood:** Needs a BORROWER role on any floating or fixed market (third party per Matt) and available credit; nothing else. Cost: gas. It is the rational move for every borrower as long as φ_T is below φ*, which it is at launch (the Wrapper is the only opted-in holder until stcUSD adoption).
**Exploit path:** (1) State: S = $100M, C = $80M, staked (stcUSD) $20M, harness curve, uw 20%. (2) Borrower draws D = $5M on a floating market (u → 80.95%, r → 10.476%). (3) Calls `Stablecoin.optIn()`; holds the cUSD. (4) Per year it receives 5/25 of the liquidity premium on all $85M of credit = $1.78M and pays (10.476% + 20%)·$5M = $1.52M: net **+$257k/yr**, repeatable up to the credit line and until φ* is reached. (5) If the borrower never repays, the underwriters are slashed for a loan whose only purpose was to farm their own premium.
**Proof:** `rate_sweep.py` §5 (rows `0.80 | 1.00 | $5M | ... -5.14% of D` and `0.90 | 1.00 | $5M | ... -61.27% of D`) and the φ* table (`0.80 | 10.00% | 20% | 0.267`). Output: `models/output/rate_sweep.txt`.
**Recommendation:** Vest the liquidity premium over the reserve-backed supply (denominator `totalSupply − creditBackedSupply`, or all holders pro-rata), or cap the staker rate at the borrower rate `r(u)`. Second-order: stcUSD yield falls to ≤ r(u) at low opt-in, which is the honest number; opting in stops being a lever.
**Invariant broken:** none listed; new candidate I41: "lender yield per cUSD ≤ borrower liquidity rate" (see Invariants).
