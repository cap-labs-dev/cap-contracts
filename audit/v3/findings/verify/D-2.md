# D-2 verification — healthy-market write-off band (`lt·(1+b) > 1e27`)

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED-AT-LOWER-SEVERITY — **Low** (Medium as filed). Mechanism, band, existence condition and all PoC numbers are correct; the loss is real and not offset by any alternative; but every step of the path is a protocol-trusted role (GUARDIAN/GOVERNOR) first choosing a parameter pair that violates the standard `lt·(1+bonus) ≤ 1` bound and then acting on a signal without the one-line health check, and the loss is bounded at ≤ 1.96 % of market collateral at the deploy bonus.

## Re-derivation (all on HEAD a843c1d)

| Quantity | Code | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| health | `BaseMarket.sol:224-234` | `h = TC·lt / D` |
| liquidate gate | `:355` | reverts `Healthy()` iff `D ≤ TC·lt` |
| recoverable | `:258-260`, `:343-346` | `R = TC / (1+b)` |
| unrecoverable | `:263-267` | `U = D − R` if `D > R` |
| write-off bound | `:380-386` | `0 < amount ≤ U`; **no** health check |
| floating `writeOff` | `FloatingMarket.sol:104-113` | `_repayWithin(D, U)` then `_writeOff` — bound by `U` only |
| fixed `writeOff(id)` | `FixedMarket.sol:151-160` | `min(debt[id], U)` then `_writeOff` — same bound |
| `setLt` | `:89-96` | accepts `(buffer, 1e27]`, no bonus read |
| `_setLiquidationBonus` | `InterestRateModel.sol:180-184` | accepts `[0, 0.1e27]`, no `lt` read |
| `Registry.initialize` | `Registry.sol:106` | same `(buffer, 1e27]` — a deployment can be *born* band-capable |

Both `U > 0` and `h ≥ 1` hold iff `TC/(1+b) < D ≤ TC·lt`, non-empty iff `lt·(1+b) > 1e27`. Thresholds: `b = 0.02 ⇒ lt > 0.98039e27`; `b = 0.1 ⇒ lt > 0.90909e27`; deploy `0.8·1.02 = 0.816` — empty. PoC numbers check: `h = 855/850 = 1.005882`, `R = 900/1.1 = 818.18`, `U = 31.82`; the "3.5 % of supply" is against a 950 supply that includes the 100 cUSD minted to the liquidator (as a fraction of the borrower's debt it is 3.74 %, of TC 3.54 %). Additional bound the finding omits: inside the band `U ≤ TC·(lt − 1/(1+b))`, i.e. **≤ 1.96 % of TC at the deploy bonus (lt = 1e27)** and ≤ 9.09 % of TC at the extreme (`lt = 1, b = 0.1`).

## Attacks on the finding, in the brief's order

1. **Precondition cannot hold on HEAD — fails.** `setLt(1e27)` is accepted. My `test_guardianOnly_bandExistsAtDeployBonus` enters the band with **GUARDIAN alone** at the untouched 2 % bonus (`lt = 1e27`, TC 505, D 500, `h = 1.01`, `U = 4.90`). GOVERNOR is not required; the finding's "b = 0.1 & lt = 0.95" is merely the widest band.
2. **Check elsewhere blocks it — fails.** Neither `writeOff` reads `healthiness()`; `_setTranches` is the only path that asserts health and it is REGISTRY-only. `test_settersIndependent_bothOrders`: `setLt(1e27)` then `setLiquidationBonus(0.1e27)` and the reverse both succeed.
3. **Numbers — correct** (above). `testFuzz_I38_holdsWhenProductAtMostOne` (256 runs, `lt ∈ [0.2,1]`, `b ∈ [0,0.1]`, `D/TC ∈ [0.05, 2]`) confirms the converse: with `lt·(1+b) ≤ 1e27` the band is empty, so the existence condition is exact, not merely sufficient.
4. **Severity — this is where it lands.**
   - *Is the write-off harmful given that liquidation "also socialises via bonus"?* **Yes, and the defence is wrong.** In the normal configuration the bonus is paid by the tranches (`_liquidate` slashes `(1+b)·repaid` from tranche capital, `:362-373`); cUSD holders only ever carry the residual after collateral is exhausted. `test_inBandWriteOff_isPureOptionTransferToBorrower` snapshots the band state and runs both counterfactuals: if the borrower repays, waiting costs cUSD holders **0** while an in-band write-off costs them exactly `U` (4.90) and the borrower pays `U` less; if instead the price crashes to $0.30 and the market is liquidated to exhaustion and written off, badDebt is **205.88 in both branches** — the early write-off changed nothing. So the in-band write-off is never better for cUSD holders and strictly worse whenever the position recovers: a pure option transfer from cUSD holders to a third-party borrower. Impact direction confirmed.
   - *"No tranche loses a token" is not band-specific.* `test/integration/DebtLifecycle.t.sol:167-205` (`test_writeOff_clearsShortfallWithTranchesUntouched`) asserts, by design, that the guardian may write off `U` with tranches still full in the *normal* configuration too. The band-specific harm is narrower than filed: the "shortfall" being written off is hypothetical (no liquidation is possible), not that tranches are spared.
   - *Likelihood.* Every actor is protocol-trusted under the engagement's trust model (curators/owners/allocators/borrowers are third parties; GUARDIAN/GOVERNOR are not). The path needs (i) GUARDIAN to raise `lt` to ≥ 0.9804 — `RoleTable.t.sol:68` documents the guardian's `setLt` as a *tightening* lever, and `lt·(1+b) > 1` is the exact configuration Aave's `PoolConfigurator` refuses (`lt·bonus ≤ 100 %`), i.e. a state where a liquidation-to-exhaustion at `h < 1` is guaranteed to leave bad debt regardless of write-off — and then (ii) GUARDIAN to call `writeOff` on `unrecoverableDebt() > 0 && maxLiquidatable() == 0` without also checking `h < 1`. The keeper-bot framing is speculative: no such bot exists in the repo, and the trigger signature it would need is one extra view. A borrower cannot force entry: with `b = 0.02`, `ltv ≤ lt − buffer ≤ 0.9 < 0.9804`; only with `b = 0.1, lt = 1, ltv = 0.9` can accrued premium alone carry `D/TC` over 0.909 without a price move, and that needs all three roles. Bounded loss (≤ 1.96 % TC at deploy bonus, ≤ 9.09 % worst case) requiring two privileged decisions, one of them a textbook misconfiguration, is a Low under the rubric; it is not a Medium.
5. **Existing coverage — none.** `test_writeOff_whenFullyCollateralised_reverts` (`DebtLifecycle.t.sol:207-218`) defines "fully collateralised" as `U == 0`, i.e. the suite itself uses the bonus-discounted bound rather than health, and runs only at deploy defaults where the band is empty. No test in `test/` sets `lt·(1+b) > 1`.

## Recommendation to the lead
**Keep, demote to Low, re-word.** The invariant gap is real and the fix is the right one (either guard in the recommendation suffices; the `healthiness() >= 1e27 ⇒ revert Healthy()` in `_writeOff` is the cheaper and closes the band regardless of parameters). Re-word the impact to drop "no tranche loses a token" as a distinguishing harm (the protocol's own tests establish that as intended in every configuration) and state the band-specific harm precisely: `unrecoverableDebt()` reports a *hypothetical* shortfall while no liquidation is possible, and acting on it is a pure transfer of up to `TC·(lt − 1/(1+b))` (≤ 1.96 % of TC at the deploy bonus) from cUSD holders to the borrower, never offset in any later branch. Re-word the likelihood to say GUARDIAN alone suffices (`setLt(1e27)` at the deploy bonus) — that is stronger for the author than the two-role path — but note that raising `lt` past `1/(1+b)` is a configuration Aave's configurator rejects outright and that the second GUARDIAN action is a one-view oversight. Cite `Registry.sol:106` as a third setter site to bound. If the lead treats GUARDIAN as fallible-by-policy (per plan §4 "safety depends on the latency of four privileged addresses"), Medium is defensible; on the stated third-party stance it is Low.
