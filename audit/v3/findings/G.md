### [Medium] G-1 — Liquidity premium is paid only to opted-in cUSD, so a borrower that parks its loan opted-in earns more than it pays whenever staked/S < u/(1 + uw/r) (P8)
**Location:** contracts/utils/PremiumVesting.sol:L114-123 (`optIn`, public, no restriction beyond self/DeadShares); contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:L83-86 (`fundCreditBacked`); contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol:L438-441 (`_chargePremium` liquidity leg)
**Impact:** Yield to a staked cUSD is `r(u)·u/φ_T` (φ_T = opted-in share of total supply) while a borrower's all-in cost is `r(u) + uw`. Whenever φ_T < u/(1+uw/r) the borrow-and-park loop is positive carry with zero capital at risk on the borrower's side (the loan is unsecured from the borrower; tranche underwriters carry it). Each loop mints credit-backed cUSD with no reserve behind it, consumes underwriter-backed credit for a circular position, and dilutes genuine stakers down to the borrower rate. At u = 0.8, harness rates (r = 10.48% after mint, uw 20%), staked $20M of $100M: a $5M parked loan nets +5.14% of D = **$257k/yr**; at u = 0.9 with the same opt-in, +61.27% of D. The loop self-limits only when staked/S reaches φ* = 26.7% (u 0.8, uw 20%), 53.3% (uw 5%), 80% (uw 0).
**Likelihood:** Needs a BORROWER role on any floating or fixed market (third party per Matt) and available credit; nothing else. Cost: gas. It is the rational move for every borrower as long as φ_T is below φ*, which it is at launch (the Wrapper is the only opted-in holder until stcUSD adoption).
**Exploit path:** (1) State: S = $100M, C = $80M, staked (stcUSD) $20M, harness curve, uw 20%. (2) Borrower draws D = $5M on a floating market (u → 80.95%, r → 10.476%). (3) Calls `Stablecoin.optIn()`; holds the cUSD. (4) Per year it receives 5/25 of the liquidity premium on all $85M of credit = $1.78M and pays (10.476% + 20%)·$5M = $1.52M: net **+$257k/yr**, repeatable up to the credit line and until φ* is reached. (5) If the borrower never repays, the underwriters are slashed for a loan whose only purpose was to farm their own premium.
**Proof:** `rate_sweep.py` §5 (rows `0.80 | 1.00 | $5M | ... -5.14% of D` and `0.90 | 1.00 | $5M | ... -61.27% of D`) and the φ* table (`0.80 | 10.00% | 20% | 0.267`). Output: `models/output/rate_sweep.txt`.
**Recommendation:** Vest the liquidity premium over the reserve-backed supply (denominator `totalSupply − creditBackedSupply`, or all holders pro-rata), or cap the staker rate at the borrower rate `r(u)`. Second-order: stcUSD yield falls to ≤ r(u) at low opt-in, which is the honest number; opting in stops being a lever.
**Invariant broken:** none listed; new candidate I41: "lender yield per cUSD ≤ borrower liquidity rate" (see Invariants).

### [Medium] G-2 — Fixed `_borrowPremium` charges the fixed borrower the rate rise on someone else's same-window floating borrow; a costless same-block floating borrow/repay overcharges any fixed draw by C·term·(r_P − r_0) (P6)
**Location:** contracts/cap/market/FixedMarket.sol:L337-351 (`_borrowPremium`, `prior = unsmoothedCredit()` L342); contracts/cap/InterestRateModel.sol:L220-224 (`unsmoothedCredit`, global, any market and type)
**Impact:** The premium is `f(prior + P)` at the after-mint rate minus `f(prior)` at the no-mint rate, so the fixed borrower pays `P·term·r_P + prior·term·(r_P − r_0)`. The second term is the rate increase its own draw causes on `prior`, which is credit it did not borrow (and which, being floating, is already paying the live rate). Harness curve, S = $100M, u = 0.8, P = $10M for 30 d: prior = $50M same block → overcharge **$17,123 = 15.15%** of the borrower's own $113,014 premium; prior = $10M → $6,227 (6.5%); at u = 0.9, prior $50M → $8,562. It decays with e^(−t/period) (1 h period: 37% remains after 1 h). The overcharge is minted to opted-in cUSD holders.
**Likelihood:** Any large floating borrow inside the averaging window triggers it organically. Adversarially, a floating BORROWER with $50M of available credit front-runs a fixed draw with `borrow(50M)` and back-runs with `repay(50M)` in the same block: `_chargePremium` returns at L171 (same timestamp) so it accrues nothing, `_repayWithin` costs ≤ 1 wei. If the attacker is opted-in with share s of staked cUSD it collects s·$17,123 per griefed draw; at s = 0 it is a free grief.
**Exploit path:** (1) State as above; victim submits `FixedMarket.borrow(10M, 30 d)`. (2) Attacker (floating borrower, opted-in, s = 50%) front-runs: `FloatingMarket.borrow(50M)`. (3) Victim's tx: `_borrowPremium` sees `prior = 50M`, charges $113,014 + $17,123. (4) Attacker back-runs: `repay(50M)`; premium accrued 0. (5) Net: victim −$17,123; attacker +$8,561 (vested over 12 h) − gas. Attacker capital: none beyond the credit line, held for one block.
**Proof:** `rate_sweep.py` §6 rows `0.80 | 5.0 | 0m | 13.333% | 13.750% | $113014 | $17123 | 15.15%` and `0.80 | 1.0 | 0m | ... $6227 | 6.49%`. Output: `models/output/rate_sweep.txt`.
**Recommendation:** Make `prior` the market's own same-window *fixed* draws (per-market accumulator reset by the average), or drop the catch-up and price `P` at `averageUtilizationAfterMint(P)` only. Keep split-invariance (I32) per market rather than globally. Second-order: two fixed draws in one window on different markets would no longer be split-invariant — acceptable, since they are different borrowers.
**Invariant broken:** I32 is split-invariant *because* of this term; new candidate I42: "a market's fixed premium for `P` is independent of other markets' same-window activity".

### [Medium] G-3 — After `unrecoverableDebt > 0`, permissionless `chargePremium` keeps minting unbacked cUSD: ~$39k/day on a $50M debt with $20M unrecoverable; +5.9% bad debt at 30 days, +82% at a year, 71% of it to the tranche about to be slashed (P9 numbers)

**Impact:** Every wei of premium on an unrecoverable debt is credit-backed supply that becomes bad debt at write-off. With both tranches already empty (`_earnsPremium` false), the full $16.4M vests on the stablecoin: opted-in cUSD holders are paid their own future write-off. A fixed market does the same per KEEPER roll: +$1.156M per 30-day `extendAdmin` (5.8% of the initial shortfall). Throughout, `Stablecoin.backing()` (L195-197) reports 100%.

**Proof:** `premium_accrual_insolvent.py` §1 (rows `30d | $51.17M | $1.170M | $39001 ...` and `365d | $66.39M | $16.385M ...`), §2, §4. Output: `models/output/premium_accrual_insolvent.txt`. Owner of P9 is WS-D; these are the numbers.
**Recommendation:** In `_chargePremium`, if `unrecoverableDebt() > 0` accrue the index but route the premium nowhere (or freeze both indices), and let anyone call `writeOff` once `unrecoverableDebt` has been positive for a bounded time. Second-order: legitimate stakers stop earning on an insolvent market — which is the honest outcome.
**Invariant broken:** I35 holds numerically; new candidate I43: "no premium is minted on a market while `unrecoverableDebt() > 0`".
