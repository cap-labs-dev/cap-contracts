Reading: unrecognised, the loss is borne 100% by the LAST ell*phi*R of redeemers (they get nothing, not a haircut),
and every earlier redeemer is paid par - the exact first-mover advantage the convex curve was built to remove.
Recognised as badDebt it is a 0.5-11.6% haircut spread monotonically along the queue.
Nothing in Stablecoin can recognise an Aera loss: coverBadDebt only lowers badDebt, writeOff is a market
action on a borrower's debt. The only exits are a donation via fund() or a permanent revert wall.

=== HEADLINE ===
invest() is invisible to unlockedSupply/maxRedeem/claimable: at the round-1 5%/day demand assumption a
one-day keeper latency means phi > 0.75 reverts the first day of redemptions (phi > 0.50 at u0 = 90%);
an Aera loss ell on phi R is paid 100% by the last ell*phi*R of redeemers as a hard zero (revert), everyone
before them at par - the first-mover advantage the haircut curve exists to remove. Keeping P(revert in 7 d)
< 1% needs phi <= 0.33 of (unlockedSupply - queue) at 1%/day demand with a 1-day recall, 0.00 at 5%/day.
Recommendation: subtract `invested` from unlockedSupply (or add a liquid-floor check in invest), and give
the loss a recognition path (invested-vs-recalled ghost -> badDebt).
