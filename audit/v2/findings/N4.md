### [MEDIUM] Circuit-breaker deleted: a feed clamped at `minAnswer` is accepted with no defence, so a collapsing collateral mints unrecoverable cUSD
**Location:** `contracts/cap/oracle/ChainlinkAdapter.sol:L10-L22` (`price`); `contracts/cap/oracle/Oracle.sol:L58-L70` (`_read`)
**Impact:** Round-1 L-15 ("bounds check ineffective") has *regressed*: the `minAnswer/maxAnswer` comparison was removed rather than fixed. When a Chainlink aggregator hits its `minAnswer` (LUNA May-2022 pattern; most legacy Ethereum/Arbitrum aggregators still carry a non-trivial `minAnswer`) it keeps stamping the clamp price fresh every heartbeat. The oracle serves it, `Tranche.totalCapital` values the collateral at the clamp, and the borrower's position stays "healthy". PoC: 2M LUNA-like collateral at a 0.1 USD clamp (true 0.0001 USD) supports 99,999 cUSD of borrowing against an asset base truly worth 200 USD; `unrecoverableDebt` at the true price is ≈ the whole loan. Loss lands on cUSD holders via `recognizeBadDebt` (the slash returns near-worthless collateral to the liquidator, who will not liquidate at all because the collateral is worth less than the bonus). Dollars at risk: the market's full credit limit for every market whose collateral feed has a `minAnswer` above zero.
**Likelihood:** Preconditions: (1) a collateral asset whose aggregator has a non-zero `minAnswer` (governance-selected; LUNA-style assets do get listed), (2) a > `minAnswer/price` crash. No attacker action is needed beyond the borrower (permissioned OPERATOR) not repaying; a rational borrower simply keeps the cUSD. Cost: zero. The event is rare but the protocol now has *no* mitigation, no pause and no way to mark the price as bad short of `setSource` to an empty chain (which reverts every `totalCapital` — M-1 open).
**Exploit path:**
1. Market with tranches in asset X, feed X/USD with `minAnswer = 0.1e8`. LPs deposit 2M X (200k USD at the clamp).
2. Borrower borrows `ltv · 200k` cUSD (test: 99,999 cUSD). State healthy.
3. X collapses to 0.0001 USD. Aggregator keeps answering `0.1e8` with a fresh `updatedAt`. `Oracle.price` = 0.1e18, `healthiness ≥ 1e27`, `unrecoverableDebt() = 0`.
4. Borrower never repays. True collateral value 200 USD. Net: 99,999 cUSD of unbacked supply; cUSD holders eat it when governance finally recognises it.
**Proof:** `N4_Oracle.t.sol::test_N8a_minAnswerClampIsAcceptedAndCreatesUnrecoverableDebt` (passes = demonstrates: `unrecoverableDebt > principal − 300e18` once the honest price is substituted, while the clamped feed is served with no error).
**Recommendation:** Reinstate a bounds check in the adapter — read `minAnswer()/maxAnswer()` from the aggregator's `aggregator()` (not the proxy) and return `(0, lastUpdated)` when `answer ≤ minAnswer` or `≥ maxAnswer`, so the hop falls to its `secondary` (the design already has a secondary slot for exactly this). Second-order: a clamped primary with no secondary yields `price == 0` → `Tranche.getPrice` reverts → market frozen (M-1); that is the intended fail-closed outcome but governance needs a documented runbook (set a secondary or `setSource` a fixed-price adapter) to unfreeze liquidations.
**Invariant broken:** new — **I25** "`Oracle.price(asset)` is never served from an aggregator round whose `answer` equals its `minAnswer`/`maxAnswer`".

### [LOW] `setSource` dry-run only checks `!= 0`: raw-feed payloads and scale errors are accepted, and a raw feed's `roundId` is served as the price
**Location:** `contracts/cap/oracle/Oracle.sol:L22-L30` (`setSource`), `L58-L70` (`_read`)
**Impact:** The dry-run is presented as the safety net for misconfiguration but it accepts (a) the aggregator itself as the adapter with `latestRoundData` as payload — `_read` decodes the first two words `(roundId, answer)` so the price becomes `roundId` (=1 in the mock, phase-encoded `2^64·phase + n` on real Chainlink feeds, i.e. an 18-dec price of ~1.8e19 USD) and `startedAt` is treated as the stamp; (b) the same 18-dec feed listed twice as a 2-hop chain (price² / 1e18 = 4,000,000 USD for ETH); (c) a 36-dec raw adapter. Any of these mis-prices every tranche in that asset by orders of magnitude and, through `totalCapital`, mints cUSD against it. Combined with L-16 a raw-feed `startedAt` far in the future is never stale.
**Likelihood:** Requires a GOVERNOR configuration mistake; no attacker. The fix is cheap and the round-1 finding on the same surface (L-15) was answered by deleting checks, so this is worth a real guard.
**Exploit path:** governance error only — none.
**Proof:** `N4_Oracle.t.sol::test_N8c_rawFeedPayloadServesRoundIdAsPrice` (price == 1), `test_N8e_dryRunAcceptsScaleErrors` (4e6e18 and 2000e36 both accepted).
**Recommendation:** In `_read` require `returnedData.length == 64` (not `≥`), and in `setSource` require the dry-run price to lie inside a governor-supplied `[min, max]` sanity band passed with the call (or against the previous chain's price ± X% when one exists).
**Invariant broken:** none listed.

### [LOW] L-16 still open: a future-dated `updatedAt` is never stale
**Location:** `contracts/cap/oracle/Oracle.sol:L72-L78` (`_isStale`)
**Impact:** `_isStale` returns `false` whenever `lastUpdated ≥ block.timestamp`, so an adapter/feed that stamps in the future serves the same price indefinitely (PoC: 364 days without an update, staleness 1 h, still served). Also disables the staleness→secondary fallback for that hop.
**Likelihood:** Needs a mis-stamping feed or adapter; Chainlink proxies do not do this, custom adapters (the design invites them) might.
**Proof:** `N4_Oracle.t.sol::test_L16_futureStampNeverStale`.
**Recommendation:** `if (_lastUpdated > _currentTimestamp) return true;` (treat future stamps as stale).

### [LOW] L-12 still open: an empty tranche (dead shares only) is permanently killed by any liquidation that passes through it
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Tranche.sol:L78-L83` (`slash` latch)
**Impact:** The new predicate `totalSupply > (total − assets)·100` evaluates to `1000 > 0` for a tranche whose LPs all left (1000 dead shares over 1000 wei), or one drained by a previous slash. `_liquidate` iterates from the junior end until `toSlash == 0`, so such a tranche receives a `slash` call, contributes 0, and is latched `killed` forever (`maxDeposit == 0`). The market owner must `createTranche` + `setTranches` to replace it. Partial slashes behave as intended (2 % of par left → not killed; < 1 % → killed).
**Likelihood:** Any liquidation on a market with an empty leading tranche. No attacker; operational nuisance.
**Proof:** `N4_KillLatchHooks.t.sol::test_L12a_dustJuniorIsKilledOnFirstSlash` (1-wei slash does not kill, full drain of the dust does, an empty tranche then returns 0 from `slash`), `test_L12b_partialSlashLeavingAbovePercentDoesNotKill`.
**Recommendation:** `if (!killed && assets != 0 && totalSupply() > (total − assets) * KILL_RATIO)` — or skip tranches with `totalAssets() == 0` in `_liquidate`. Note `assets == 0` also covers the never-funded tranche (`totalSupply == 0`) which today survives only because `0 > 0` is false.

### [INFORMATIONAL] N9 — hooks from a collateral token inside `liquidate` can reach `Tranche.deposit/optOut/claim/requestRedeem` and `Stablecoin.deposit`; none breaks accounting, instant redeem is blocked by the lock
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Tranche.sol:L85` (`IVault(vault).withdraw` inside `slash`), `contracts/cap/Vault.sol:L40-L43`
**Findings (each is a running test in `N4_KillLatchHooks.t.sol`):**
- `Vault.withdraw` burns the ERC-6909 balance *before* `safeTransfer`, so when the hook runs `Tranche.totalAssets()` has already dropped and `killed` is already set. `deposit` from the hook mints at the **post-slash** price (`test_N9_depositFromHookMidSlashSeesPostSlashPrice`, exact to 2 wei). No mid-slash price to exploit.
- Instant `redeem` on the junior *and* on the not-yet-slashed senior is refused with `ERC4626ExceededMaxRedeem(…, 0)`: `lockedValue = debt/(lt − buffer)` exceeds tranche capital whenever the market is at or below `targetHealth`, and `_repay` (before the loop) only restores to `targetHealth` at `maxLiquidatable`. The "exit the senior at the pre-slash price" path therefore needs `targetHealth > lt/(lt − buffer)`; not reachable with the deployed parameters (`test_N9_seniorRedeemFromHookEscapesItsOwnSlash` logs `maxRedeem == 0` after a max liquidation). Flag for N3/lead: if governance ever sets `targetHealth` above that ratio, an LP whose collateral has a transfer hook can front-run its own slash within the liquidation tx.
- `optOut`, `claim`, `Stablecoin.deposit` all succeed from the hook; `stakedSupply == Σ opted-in balances` and `balanceOf(tranche) == redemptionQueue` hold afterwards (`test_N9_requestRedeemOptOutClaimFromHook`). `requestRedeem` failed only because the test token is not an ERC-1155 receiver — it is otherwise unguarded.
- Restricted entry points (`Underwriter.allocate/deallocate/report`, `Tranche.fund`, `mintCreditBacked`) are unreachable from a hook without a role, so "`deallocate` mid-slash" is not a hook scenario; a curator contract with a hooked collateral could do it, but `slash` reads no Underwriter state.
- Read-only reentrancy: `totalCapital/healthiness/previewRedeem` are consistent at hook time (assets already burnt, debt already repaid) — the only inconsistency is that *later* tranches in the loop are still un-slashed, which is the design's waterfall order, not a stale view.
**Recommendation:** none required; consider `nonReentrant` on `Tranche.deposit/mint` for belt-and-braces if hooked collateral is ever whitelisted.

### [INFORMATIONAL] I22 / L-17 — EMA is path-independent; `rayPow` error is O(n) ulp
- `rayPow(r, n)` vs a 36-digit floor reference: error grows with `n` because every squaring doubles the accumulated half-up rounding — **1713 ulp (1.7e-24) observed at n = 28,013**; bounded by `n + 64` ulp and by 1e-18 absolute for all `n ≤ 3.15e8` (10 y) and all `period ∈ [5 min, 1 day]` (`testFuzz_rayPowAccuracy`, 5,000 runs). Never reverts, `≤ RAY`, monotone non-increasing in `n` for any base `≤ RAY` (`testFuzz_rayPowBoundedAndMonotone`, 5,000 runs); overflow impossible since `c, a ≤ RAY` at every step. Underflow to 0 happens only where the exact value is < 0.5e-27 (5-min period: n ≈ 18.6 k; 1-day period at 10 y: exactly 0) — the "skip the last squaring" comment is correct and the early-underflow it guards against does not occur. Gas: `rayPow(r, 3e8)` = **7,208**.
- EMA (`_carry` and `_averagingWeight` reproduced verbatim): one-shot vs `k` random splits over `elapsed ∈ [1, 10 d]`, `period ∈ [5 min, 1 d]`, `k ∈ [1, 200]`, random `avg0/observed` up to 2^128: |difference| ≤ `1 + (k + elapsed + 64)·(gap/1e27 + 1)` wei (`testFuzz_I22_emaPathIndependent`, 5,000 runs). For realistic supplies (≤ 1e30 wei) the divergence is ≤ ~1e6 wei = 1e-12 cUSD, cannot be steered upward by the choice of `k` beyond that linear bound, and is always symmetric noise (not a bias). L-17 is fixed.

## Invariants
- I22 holds (tolerance above). No plan invariant broken by this workstream.
- New: **I25** `Oracle.price(asset) ≠ f(minAnswer)` — an aggregator answering exactly its `minAnswer`/`maxAnswer` must not be served (broken today, Medium above).
- New: **I26** `_read` accepts only exactly-64-byte returns (broken today, Low above).
- New: **I27** a tranche with `totalAssets() == 0` is never latched `killed` by `slash` (broken today, Low above).

## Appendix: gas & style
- `rayPow` 10-year exponent costs 7.2 k gas; per-transfer cost in `PremiumVesting._update` is dominated by storage, not the pow.
- `Oracle._read` decodes `(uint256,uint256)` from any `≥ 64`-byte return; `abi.decode` with a fixed length would be strictly safer at no cost.
