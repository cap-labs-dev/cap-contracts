## Verdict: DEMOTE to Low

## Why (one paragraph, the decisive reason)
The mechanism is real (`maxRedeem`/`maxWithdraw`/`claimableRedeemRequest` are computed from `unlockedSupply()`, which is a supply-side number, not the token balance; `_transferOut` then reverts when `balanceOf(stablecoin) < assets`), but it is reachable only through the privileged KEEPER role (`Registry.sol:344-348`, granted at `ConfigureAccessControl.sol:20`), it produces no loss, and it is not even a keeper-dependent freeze: a reverted claim leaves `queueIndex`, `settledQueue`, the ERC-1155 receipt and `claimableRedeemRequest` untouched, and the claim settles in full after either a `recall` **or any unrelated fresh deposit** (the liquid reserve is fungible), so the worst case is a delay bounded by keeper latency or organic inflow. `IStablecoin` NatSpec frames the reserve vault as holding "idle reserve" that the "Keeper can recall", i.e. the "recall before large claims settle" assumption is the accepted design. Under Immunefi-style scoring a temporary, privileged-role-gated freeze with no extractable value is Low; the ERC-4626 `maxRedeem` overstatement is a genuine spec deviation that should be fixed (clamp by liquid balance, or JIT `recall` in `_transferOut`), but no on-chain integrator in this repo consumes `Stablecoin.maxRedeem`/`redeem` (`Underwriter` redeems `Tranche`, not cUSD; there is no `Wrapper` contract under `contracts/` at 3dad5ef), so the integrator-breakage impact is hypothetical.

## Corrections to the finding text (bullet list, or "none")
- "no partial fill" → the contract does not clamp, but the caller can always claim/redeem up to the liquid balance (the PoC's own 999 cUSD step proves it); rephrase as "no automatic clamp; caller must size the call to the liquid balance".
- "blocks it until a discretionary `recall`" → also unblocked by any subsequent deposit/`fund` inflow (verify test 3); the freeze is on the *marginal* redeemer, not on a specific claim.
- "the `Wrapper`" as a broken integrator → no such contract exists under `contracts/` at 3dad5ef; cite only external 4626 routers/off-chain integrators.
- Round-1 identity note is correct (`liquid + aeraHeld == unlockedSupply()`); keep.
- Severity Medium → Low. Keep recommendation (2) (clamp views by liquid balance or JIT `recall` in an overridden `_transferOut`) as the fix; recommendation (1) (cap `invest` below `liquid − queuedAssets`) is the cheap interim.
