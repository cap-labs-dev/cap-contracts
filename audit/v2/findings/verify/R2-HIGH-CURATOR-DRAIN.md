# R2-HIGH-CURATOR-DRAIN — verification of N3-1

## Verdict: CONFIRMED

## Why (one paragraph, the decisive reason)
The PoC runs unmodified on the production wiring (`registry.createUnderwriter` → `_configureUnderwriterRoles`, no test-deployer shortcut) and moves 100% of the underwriter's Vault balance to a curator EOA with nothing but the curator operator role: `Underwriter.addTranche` (Underwriter.sol:78-87) does no check on its argument and calls `IVault(vault).setOperator(_tranche, true)`, and `Vault.transferFrom` inherits ERC-7540/6909 operator semantics with no override, so any contract that implements `optIn()` can `transferFrom(underwriter, …)` every id. This is not a privileged-role-does-privileged-thing case that Immunefi scoring would exclude: the protocol's own trust model at the immediately preceding commit (3c45dca, Registry.sol:426-435) states verbatim that "Registration is held above the curator on purpose. addTranche hands its argument vault operator rights over the underwriter's entire balance … so a curator holding it could register a contract of their own and move the balance out", and 3dad5ef — commit message "Refactor premium vesting and natspec", no NatSpec or rationale replacing the deleted comment — moved the selector to the curator role and deleted that comment. That is a regression against a documented invariant, not a re-decided trust boundary. The curator has no comparable pre-existing theft path: allocated shares live in the Tranche (ERC-4626), not the Vault, so operator rights do not reach them, and "allocate into a market I borrow from and default" needs KEEPER to pair the same operator as borrower/owner, needs the market owner to admit the underwriter as depositor, and is bounded by `ltv` and the waterfall — nowhere near unilateral 100% loss. High stands.

Angle (b) — pre-existing curator theft surface: `createFloatingMarket` (Registry.sol:248-250) and `createUnderwriter` (:176) each look up `operatorRole[account]`, so one account *can* be curator and borrower, but only if KEEPER creates both with that account and the market owner admits the underwriter into the tranche depositor role. That path is capped at `ltv` of the allocated capital and goes through liquidation/slash, versus N3-1's unilateral, instant, 100% of idle + deallocatable balance. It does not lower severity.

## Corrections to the finding text (bullet list, or "none")
- "Irreversible at the curator level: `removeTranche` reverts on a non-tranche … so the operator grant can only be undone by an ADMIN upgrade" — true only for a fake that does not answer `balanceOf/previewRedeem/claim`. A curator who wants to cover tracks stubs those three and `removeTranche` succeeds (verify test 4a). Reword to: revocation is at the fake's discretion; either way the balance is already gone, and revoking the curator's *role* does not clear the Vault operator flag (4c).
- Trust-model table row "addTranche with a typo'd address: operator right granted to garbage, removeTranche reverts on it" — unreachable: `addTranche` reverts on any no-code address because of `IPremiumVesting(_tranche).optIn()` (4b). The argument must be a contract implementing `optIn()`; the risk is a malicious or mistakenly-chosen *contract*, not a typo.
- Location cite `Underwriter.sol:79-88` → `:78-87`; `Registry.sol:392-401` → `:444-455` at 3dad5ef.
- Recommendation (3) "move `addTranche`/`removeTranche` back above the curator" is the exact 3c45dca wiring (`CapRoles.ADMIN`); cite that commit so the fix is a revert plus restoring the deleted comment.

## Residual doubt (what would settle it if still uncertain)
Whether 3dad5ef's move was a deliberate product decision ("curators pick their own tranches") that simply forgot to add the registry-membership check, or an accidental regression while removing `setVestingPeriod` from the same array. Either way the exploit and the loss are identical; only the wording of the root cause changes. A one-line confirmation from the author of 3dad5ef would settle it. If the intent is for curators to self-serve registration, the fix is recommendation (1)/(2) (registry `isTranche` + per-allocation allowance) rather than (3).
