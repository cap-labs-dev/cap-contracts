## Verdict: CONFIRMED

## Why (one paragraph, the decisive reason)
The mechanism is real, reachable under production wiring, irreversible by any protocol role, and produces a loss that was not reachable by the market owner at 3c45dca. `Registry._configureMarketRoles` (`Registry.sol:375-382`) puts `setTranches` on the per-market owner operator role with no GOVERNOR/ADMIN in the loop; `_setTranches` (`BaseMarket.sol:346-362`) checks only `market()`, uniqueness, `Σ weights == 1e27` and `healthiness() ≥ 1e27`, and `_liquidate` (`BaseMarket.sol:311-331`) slashes strictly by array index, so any tranche the owner leaves out (or moves to a lower index) leaves the first-loss position. Weights never touch the waterfall (they are only read in `_chargePremium`), so at 3c45dca the owner's worst case was `setLtv(lt-buffer)`: the junior still absorbed the first loss and the senior's par exposure topped out at `1120·1.02 − 600 = 542` (54%) — my `test_A3` shows the junior wiped and the senior left with 737 at ltv 0.7. After `3dad5ef` the owner can void the first-loss layer entirely: with the PoC's numbers a 30% collateral drop leaves the untouched market healthy (nobody slashed, `test_A1`), while the stripped market has the senior slashed to 0 and the junior recovering 600 of 600 after the residual is written off (`test_A2`). A reorder `[junior, senior]` passes with health unchanged at 1.6 and makes the senior first-loss and fully locked (unlocked 457 → 0) without any exit for the junior having been needed (`test_B`), which shows the harm is about slash-loop membership and order, not the 1.6 → 1.0 cushion. The trigger is a privileged operator (GOVERNOR-onboarded), which is why this is not High; but the protocol's own trust model held this selector at ADMIN in v1 and the round-1 table records the owner's worst case as "no direct withdrawal", senior LPs have no veto and the strip cannot be undone by GUARDIAN, GOVERNOR or ADMIN (`test_F`), so it is a genuine regression of the owner's trust boundary with real, bounded LP loss — Medium stands.

## Corrections to the finding text (bullet list, or "none")
- "senior's loss on a full default rises from 20% to 80%" overstates the *delta attributable to this change*: at 3c45dca the owner could already push senior par exposure to `1120·1.02 − 600 = 542` (54%) via `setLtv(lt−buffer)` and a max borrow (`test_A3`). The correct framing is: the junior first-loss layer becomes voidable (junior loss 600 → 0, senior 216 → 816 at par), and for a self-underwritten borrower the default profit becomes `≈ lt·S − residual ≈ 0.66·S` regardless of how much junior it posted (at v1 it was `0.7·S − 0.3·J`, so a large junior stake was a real deterrent). That is the qualitative regression.
- The "only check is health ≥ 1.0 at `lt`" framing is misleading: a reorder `[junior, senior]` passes with health *unchanged* at 1.6 (`test_B`) and is arguably worse for the senior (first-loss and fully locked, unlocked 457 → 0, with no exit for anyone). The recommendation "check health at `ltv`" would not stop it. The gate that matters is membership *and index* of funded tranches while `totalDebt() > 0`.
- Drop owner == borrower from the headline; the PoC itself uses owner ≠ borrower and the harm needs only a malicious or careless owner. Owner == borrower (F-I6) only changes who profits.
- "The 143 residual left in the junior is locked but unslashable until the debt is repaid (dead capital)" — correct while the debt stands, but it is not permanently dead: it unlocks fully once the residual is written off by GUARDIAN or repaid (`test_A2`: junior redeems 600 of 600). Rephrase as "temporarily locked, never slashable".
- I26 should also cover order: while `totalDebt() > 0`, no tranche with live supply may leave `tranches()` **or move to a lower index** except through GOVERNOR/ADMIN. Recommendation: in `_setTranches`, for each tranche in the *old* array with `totalSupply() > DeadShares.SHARES`, require it to be present in the new array at an index ≥ its old index whenever `totalDebt() > 0`; or return removal/reorder to GOVERNOR and leave only `setTrancheWeights` + append (`createTranche`) at the owner. `createTranche` appends with old tranches in place, so an append-only rule is compatible.
- Severity Medium: keep.

## Residual doubt (what would settle it if still uncertain)
Whether the team *intends* the market owner to be fully trusted by its own tranche depositors (in which case this is a documentation item, not a finding). The move from ADMIN to owner in `3dad5ef` carries only a plumbing comment ("this contract calls setTranches from createFloatingMarket / createTranche") and no docs or NatSpec claim that removal of a funded tranche is an owner power; a one-line statement from the authors that senior depositors are expected to trust the owner with waterfall membership would demote this to Informational (trust-model note alongside F-I6). Absent that, Medium.
