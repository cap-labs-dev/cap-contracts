## Findings

### [HIGH] N3-1 A curator registers any address as a "tranche" and drains 100% of the underwriter's vault balance
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Underwriter.sol:79-88` (`addTranche`), `contracts/cap/Registry.sol:392-401` (`_configureUnderwriterRoles`, `addTranche` → curator operator role), `contracts/cap/Vault.sol` (OZ ERC-6909 `setOperator`: operator may `transferFrom` any id, any amount)
**Impact:** every depositor's idle balance in the Underwriter leaves in one call; the allocated portion is reachable too (curator `deallocate`s first, which returns tranche shares to the underwriter's vault balance). Depositor shares quote at 0 afterwards (`previewRedeem == 0`). 100% of underwriter TVL. Irreversible at the curator level: `removeTranche` reverts on a non-tranche (`_report → _mark → balanceOf` on the fake), so the operator grant can only be undone by an ADMIN upgrade.
**Likelihood:** attacker must hold the underwriter's curator operator role (granted by GOVERNOR `assignOperator` + KEEPER `createUnderwriter`). Cost: gas. No user interaction, no timing, no oracle. The v1 Registry held `addTranche` above the curator for exactly this reason (round-1 report §3, F.md trust model: "hands its argument vault operator rights over the underwriter's entire balance and does not check that the address is a tranche this registry deployed"). `3dad5ef` moved the selector to the curator role and kept the unchecked argument.
**Exploit path:**
1. Alice and Bob deposit 1,000 + 500 collateral into the underwriter (TVL 1,500).
2. Curator deploys `Drain` (`optIn()` no-op, `pull()` = `IVault.transferFrom(underwriter, curator, asset, balanceOf)`).
3. Curator calls `addTranche(drain)` — `_registeredTranches.add`, `vault.setOperator(drain, true)`, `drain.optIn()` — nothing checks `drain`.
4. `drain.pull()` moves 1,500e18; curator `vault.withdraw`s to plain ERC-20. `uw.totalAssets() == 0`, `previewRedeem(alice) == 0`.
5. Allocated funds: curator `deallocate(tranche, all)` first (no debt → fully unlocked; with debt, the instant-unlocked part plus `deallocateAsync` over time), then step 4. Net: 100% of TVL; residual `totalDebt` mark is the only thing left.
**Proof:** `N3_CuratorDrain.t.sol` — `test_N1_curatorDrainsIdleBalance` (moved 1500000000000000000000 wei), `test_N1_allocatedPortionIsReachableToo` (idle after deallocate 1499999999999999999000, then drained), `test_N1_removeTrancheDoesNotHelpAfterTheFact`.
**Recommendation:** (1) `Registry` records every tranche it deploys (`mapping(address => bool) isTranche`, set in `_deployTranche`) and `addTranche` requires `IRegistry(registry).isTranche(_tranche) && ITranche(_tranche).asset() == asset()` — Underwriter needs the registry address at init; (2) do not use the blanket ERC-6909 operator: `Vault.approve(tranche, id(asset), amount)` per allocation inside `_allocate`, revoked to 0 after the deposit, so even a registered tranche can only pull what the curator is allocating; (3) until then, move `addTranche`/`removeTranche` back above the curator (GOVERNOR). Second-order: (2) makes `Tranche.deposit → _transferIn` rely on allowance instead of operator status — `_fundUnderwriter`/`_fundTranche` in tests use `setOperator`; keep both paths or migrate tests.
**Invariant broken:** new — **I25** `∀ o ∈ Vault.isOperator(underwriter, o): Registry.isTranche(o)` (never held; no code asserts it).

### [MEDIUM] N3-2 Market OWNER strips a funded junior tranche out of the waterfall after the borrow; junior exits, senior absorbs the whole default
**Location:** `contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol:104-107, 346-362` (`setTranches`/`_setTranches`), `:237-251` (`lockedValue` — a tranche not in the array falls out of the loop without `break`), `contracts/cap/Registry.sol:302-310` (`setTranches` at owner role; Registry grants itself the owner role), `_createMarket` (owner == borrower permitted)
**Impact:** the only checks are `Σ weights == 1e27` and `healthiness() ≥ 1e27` at `lt`. With senior 1,000 / junior 600 / debt 800 (ltv 0.5), the owner sets `tranches = [senior]`: passes at exactly 1.0. The removed junior now reads `lockedValue = max(0, debt/(lt−buffer) − Σ in-array capital) = 143` so 457 of its 600 is instantly redeemable, and it is no longer in the slash loop at all. Any price tick makes the market liquidatable with the senior tranche first (and only). On a full default the loss shifts from junior-600/senior-200 to junior-0/senior-800: the senior LP's loss goes from 20% to 80% of its capital. The 143 residual left in the junior is locked but unslashable until the debt is repaid (dead capital). Baseline round 1: `setTranches` was ADMIN.
**Likelihood:** holder of the market owner operator role (GOVERNOR-onboarded, KEEPER-created). `Registry` allows `_marketOwner == _borrower` (same operator role), so a self-underwritten market's owner can borrow at `ltv`, pull its own junior capital out from under the loan, and default. Cost: gas; profit = the junior capital that would otherwise have been slashed (457 of 600 in the PoC). Senior LPs have no veto and ADMIN is not in the loop.
**Exploit path:** 1. owner/borrower funds junior 600, third party funds senior 1,000; 2. borrow max = 800; 3. `setTranches([senior, 1e27])` (health 1.0 exactly, accepted); 4. `redeem(457)` from the junior; 5. let price move 1 bp / stop paying — `healthiness < 1e27`, `tranches().length == 1`, senior is slashed for the full shortfall.
**Proof:** `N3_OwnerWaterfall.t.sol` — `test_N7_ownerRemovesFundedJuniorTrancheAfterBorrow` (junior unlocked 0 → 457142857142857142857; lockedValue(junior) 142857142857142857143; healthiness 1.6e27 → 1.0e27), `test_N7_registryAllowsOwnerEqualsBorrower`, `test_N7_seniorLpCannotVetoAndAdminIsNotInvolved`. Stateful fuzz `N3_OwnerWaterfall.invariants.t.sol` (W1–W3, 4 × 128,000 calls, 0 reverts): the owner alone can never go *below* 1.0 (W1/W3 hold) — the exposure is exactly "to 1.0 and out of the loop", not undercollateralisation.
**Recommendation:** `_setTranches` should refuse to drop a tranche with `totalSupply() > DeadShares.SHARES` while `totalDebt() > 0` (or require the dropped tranche's `unlockedSupply() == totalSupply()` under the *old* array), and should check health at `ltv` (the borrow line), not `lt` (the liquidation line). Keep `createTranche`/append at the owner; gate removal/reorder on GOVERNOR or on `totalDebt() == 0`. Also either forbid `_marketOwner == _borrower` in `_createMarket` or document it as a trusted configuration. Second-order: `createTranche` calls `setTranches` with the old tranches in place, so an append-only rule does not break it.
**Invariant broken:** new — **I26** while `totalDebt() > 0`, the set of tranches in `tranches()` with `totalSupply() > 0` is non-decreasing except through ADMIN/GOVERNOR.

### [LOW] N3-3 (L-8 residual) The production deploy path still cannot be broadcast, and it only works when the deploying contract is the AccessManager's initial admin
**Location:** `contracts/deploy/service/DeployInfra.sol:64-66, 96-99` (`VM.getNonce(address(this))`, `computeCreateAddress(address(this), …)` for IRM, Stablecoin and now Registry; `grantRole(ADMIN, registryAddr)` from `address(this)`), `ConfigureAccessControl.sol`
**Impact:** `forge script … --broadcast` (and plain `forge script`, which simulates broadcast) is refused before the IRM is deployed: `Usage of address(this) detected in script contract`. Separately, `_deployInfra` grants ADMIN/REGISTRY to the precomputed Registry from `address(this)`; with `users.admin` ≠ the deploying contract (a multisig) this reverts `AccessManagerUnauthorizedAccount(deployer, 0)` — and when it does work, the deploying contract keeps ADMIN forever (nothing renounces it; `_initInfraAccessControl` then grants ADMIN to `users.admin` *in addition*). `foundry.toml` still points `script = "scripts"` (no such directory; the real `script/` tree is off the build path) while the deploy code lives in `contracts/deploy`, reachable only from tests.
**Likelihood:** hits every production deployment attempt; no attacker.
**Exploit path:** n/a (operational).
**Proof:** `N3_ProdDeployBroadcast.s.sol` (ported round-1 F6): `forge script … --sender 0x1000…0001 -vvv` → `[Revert] Usage of address(this) detected in script contract.` after `ChainlinkAdapter` is created. `N3_UUPS_RoleTable.t.sol::test_L8_deployInfraRequiresDeployerToBeInitialAdmin` (revert `AccessManagerUnauthorizedAccount(this, 0)`).
**Recommendation:** compute addresses from `users.deployer` (`vm.getNonce(users.deployer)`) or, better, deploy the Registry proxy *uninitialised* (`ERC1967Proxy(impl, "")` → grant ADMIN/REGISTRY → `initialize`) so no address precomputation is needed; pass the IRM/Stablecoin circularity the same way. Have the deployer renounce ADMIN at the end of `_initInfraAccessControl` when `users.admin != deployer`. Second-order: an uninitialised Registry proxy is front-runnable between creation and `initialize` — do the three steps in one transaction (a deploy helper contract) or keep the atomic `ERC1967Proxy(impl, data)` and precompute from the real sender.

### [INFORMATIONAL] N3-4 UUPS inherited by beacon-deployed contracts is dead, misleading surface (N10 — no exploit)
**Location:** `Tranche.sol:210`, `Underwriter.sol` (end), `BaseMarket.sol:436` — `UUPSUpgradeable` + `_authorizeUpgrade … restricted`; instances are `BeaconProxy`s via `BeaconFactory`
**Impact:** none exploitable. Verified: `upgradeToAndCall` on every beacon instance and on every bare implementation reverts `UUPSUnauthorizedCallContext`; `proxiableUUID()` on an instance reverts the same way (`notDelegated`), so OZ upgrade tooling that probes it will misreport the instance as non-UUPS — correct, but the `_authorizeUpgrade` role-0 rows in the manager are noise. Implementations have `_disableInitializers`; `Tranche(impl).initialize` and `Registry(impl).initialize` revert `InvalidInitialization`. `Registry.initialize` runs inside the `ERC1967Proxy` creation transaction, so it cannot be front-run; a missing ADMIN grant makes it revert (redeploy, no state).
**Proof:** `N3_UUPS_RoleTable.t.sol::test_N10_upgradeToAndCallRevertsOnBeaconInstancesAndImplementations`.
**Recommendation:** drop `UUPSUpgradeable` from `Tranche`, `Underwriter`, `BaseMarket` (upgrades go through the four `UpgradeableBeacon`s owned by `users.admin`). Note the beacons are `Ownable` by `users.admin` directly — outside the AccessManager, so the manager's role table does not show the most powerful upgrade path.

### [INFORMATIONAL] N3-5 I24 holds on the production path; residual trust-model notes (Wrapper undeployed, Registry holds ADMIN + every owner role, deployer never renounces)
**Location:** `Registry._configure*`, `DeployInfra`, `ConfigureAccessControl`
**Impact:** 61 restricted selectors across Registry, Vault, Stablecoin, IRM, Oracle, BeaconFactory, a floating market, a fixed market, a tranche and an underwriter resolve to a named role; the only role-0 rows are `upgradeToAndCall` (ADMIN by design — same for Stablecoin/IRM/Oracle/BeaconFactory). Full table below. Residuals: (a) `Wrapper` (the stcUSD-equivalent) is not deployed by `DeployInfra` at all — whoever deploys it must initialise it against the same manager or its `upgradeToAndCall` is admin-less; (b) `Registry` permanently holds ADMIN, REGISTRY and *every* market owner role (`_configureMarketRoles` grants `ownerRole` to itself) — an upgrade of the Registry implementation is therefore an upgrade of a super-operator; (c) the deploying contract keeps ADMIN (N3-3).
**Proof:** `N3_UUPS_RoleTable.t.sol::test_I24_roleTable` (log in the test output; `role-0 rows: 2`).
**Recommendation:** add `Wrapper` to `DeployInfra`; document (b); renounce (c).

### [INFORMATIONAL] N3-6 `ReentrancyGuardTransient` requires EIP-1153 on every listed target chain; `foundry.toml` pins no `evm_version` (N11)
**Location:** `contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol` (inherits `ReentrancyGuardTransient`), `foundry.toml` (`solc 0.8.36`, no `evm_version`, RPCs: ethereum, monad, tempo, megaeth, katana)
**Impact:** every `nonReentrant` market entry point (borrow, repay, liquidate, chargePremium, all setters) reverts on a chain without `TSTORE`/`TLOAD`. The solc 0.8.36 default target is post-Cancun, so PUSH0 and MCOPY are also emitted. To my knowledge all four non-Ethereum targets advertise Cancun-level opcode support (Monad: Cancun opcodes incl. TLOAD/TSTORE/MCOPY, blob opcodes excluded; MegaETH and Tempo: reth-based, Cancun/Prague; Katana: OP Stack post-Ecotone) — treat this as a claim to re-verify against each chain's current docs before deploying; the `--sender`-refused deploy script (N3-3) means no chain has been exercised yet.
**Recommendation:** set `evm_version` explicitly per target profile; add a deploy-time probe (a contract whose constructor executes `tstore/tload` and reverts otherwise) as the first transaction of `DeployInfra`.

### Per-role worst case (roles that gained power in `3dad5ef`)

| Role | Worst honest mistake | Worst malicious action |
|---|---|---|
| Curator (underwriter operator) | `addTranche` with a typo'd address: operator right granted to garbage, `removeTranche` reverts on it (irrevocable without ADMIN); `setDefaultTranche` to an unhealthy market. | **N3-1**: register a contract of their own, drain 100% of TVL incl. the allocated part after `deallocate`. Also: allocate everything to a market they borrow from. |
| Market owner | `setTranches` with the wrong order/subset while a loan is open — a funded tranche silently leaves the slash loop; `createTranche` for an asset with a weak feed (only `price != 0` is checked — governor gate). | **N3-2**: as owner == borrower, borrow at `ltv`, drop own junior tranche, redeem it, default on the senior LP. Also `setLtv`/`setUnderwriterRate`/`setMarketMultiplier` to the bounds in one block. |
| Keeper | `invest` too much of the reserve (N2 — redemptions revert until `recall`); `createUnderwriter` with the wrong asset; `report` never called (stale marks, H-1). | `invest(all)` to DoS every cUSD redemption; create markets pairing any two GOVERNOR-assigned operators with any priced assets/weights; `extendAdmin` any fixed loan. Cannot self-assign an operator role. |

## Invariants
- **I24** holds on the production path (only `upgradeToAndCall` rows at role 0, by design).
- **I25 (new, broken):** `∀ o : Vault.isOperator(underwriter, o) ⇒ Registry-deployed tranche with the same asset` — N3-1.
- **I26 (new, broken):** while `totalDebt() > 0`, no tranche with live supply leaves `tranches()` without GOVERNOR/ADMIN — N3-2.
- **W1–W3** (fuzzed, hold): owner actions alone keep `healthiness ≥ 1e27`, `Σ_in-array capital · lt ≥ debt`; a removed tranche reads `lockedValue = max(0, debt/(lt−buffer) − Σ in-array capital)` and is never slashed.
