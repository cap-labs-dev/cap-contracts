### [HIGH] An Aera reserve loss is never recognised: share price stays at par and the whole loss lands on the last redeemers
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:103-118` (`invest`, `recall`), `:170-180` (`unlockedSupply`, `totalAssets`), `:146-153` (`recognizeBadDebt`, MARKET-only)
**Impact:** `Stablecoin` keeps no record of what it sent to Aera. `totalAssets() = totalSupply − badDebt`, `unlockedSupply()`, `previewRedeem`, `maxRedeem` and `claimableRedeemRequest` all continue to promise par after any shortfall on the invested leg (strategy loss, Aera TVL/performance fee accrual, a guardian trade at a bad price, or a compromised guardian draining the vault). The only haircut mechanism (`badDebt` → `_convertToAssets` squared-haircut) is written solely by markets via `recognizeBadDebt`, which also does `creditBackedSupply -= amount` and therefore cannot honestly book a reserve loss (it would corrupt utilization). Result is a classic bank run on an invested reserve: early redeemers exit at par, the last `loss` worth of holders hold cUSD with zero backing and every view function still reports them covered. Dollars at risk: 100 % of the Aera shortfall, borne by whoever redeems last, unbounded by any cap on `invest`.
**Likelihood:** Preconditions: KEEPER has invested any amount and the Aera vault returns less than it took. No attacker is needed for the slow case — if the deployed Aera vault is a V3 `FeeVault` derivative its fee claim alone guarantees a monotone leak (verify against the actual vault; the interface in `IAeraVault.sol` does not expose fees or valuation). The fast case needs an Aera guardian compromise or a bad strategy, both outside Cap's trust boundary. Cost to Cap's users: unrecoverable, because nothing in the contract can even represent the loss.
**Exploit path (bank-run model, `test_N2b_aeraLossLandsOnLastRedeemer`):**
1. Alice and Bob deposit 500 each; `invest(500)` (50 % of reserve).
2. Aera loses 20 % (100); keeper `recall(400)` — the system now holds 900 against `unlockedSupply = totalAssets = 1000`, `badDebt = 0`, `previewRedeem(1e18) = 1e18`.
3. Alice `redeem(500)` at par.
4. Bob: `maxRedeem = 500`, `redeem(500)` reverts (`ERC20InsufficientBalance`), `redeem(400)` succeeds, Bob keeps 100 cUSD backed by nothing while `totalAssets()` still reports 100.
5. `test_N2b_noRecognitionPath`: `recognizeBadDebt(100)` reverts (underflow, no credit) and, with credit outstanding, silently mislabels the reserve loss as a borrower write-off; `coverBadDebt` reverts `NoBadDebt`. There is no path.
**Proof:** `N2.t.sol::test_N2b_aeraLossLandsOnLastRedeemer`, `test_N2b_noRecognitionPath` (both encode the defect as assertions and pass on current code). Stateful: `N2.invariants.t.sol::N2LossInvariants` — `invariant_lossGapEqualsGhostLoss_neverRecognised` and `invariant_sharePriceStillParDespiteLoss` hold over **256 runs × 500 depth = 128,000 handler calls, 0 reverts** (9,094 `aeraLose` calls): `unlockedSupply() == liquid + aeraHeld + ghostLoss` exactly, `totalAssets()` overstates backing by exactly `ghostLoss`, and the share price never moves — i.e. the loss is provably invisible to every accounting surface.
**Recommendation:** (a) Track `invested` in storage (`+= amount` on `invest`, `−= amount` on `recall`) so the contract knows its claim on Aera. (b) Add a `recognizeReserveLoss(uint256)` (ADMIN/KEEPER, or permissionless against an Aera valuation feed) that does `badDebt += loss; invested −= loss` **without** touching `creditBackedSupply`, so the existing squared-haircut in `_convertToAssets` and the `_onWithdraw` retirement apply to reserve losses exactly as to credit losses. (c) Make `recall` self-reporting: if Aera delivers less than requested (or its `withdraw` reverts on a partial), book the difference. Second-order: `unlockedSupply` subtracts `badDebt`, so booking a reserve loss shrinks the settled queue window (queued claims regress) and every holder takes a pro-rata haircut instead of the last one taking all of it — that is the intended semantics. It also raises utilization (`totalSupply` unchanged, but `unlockedSupply` down) only through the liquidity-rate path; check IRM inputs. Until (b) exists, the safe interim is a hard cap on `invest` (see next finding) sized to what governance is willing to socialise.
**Invariant broken:** I1 (reserve identity), and the core promise "cUSD redeemable at `previewRedeem`".

### [MEDIUM] `invest` breaks the ERC-4626/7540 view guarantees: `maxRedeem`, `maxWithdraw` and `claimableRedeemRequest` overstate what can be paid, so instant redeems and settled queued claims revert on `safeTransfer`
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:103-110` (`invest`, no bound); `contracts/ERC7540/ERC7540AsyncRedeem.sol:155-159` (`maxRedeem`), `:115-133` (`claimableRedeemRequest`), `:229/:249` (`_withdraw` → `_transferOut`)
**Impact:** After `invest(x)` with liquid reserve R, `maxRedeem`/`maxWithdraw`/`claimableRedeemRequest` are unchanged but only `R − x` can be transferred. There is no partial fill: any single redeem or claim for more than the liquid balance reverts atomically; a redeemer can only succeed by guessing the liquid balance off-chain. ERC-4626 requires `maxRedeem` "MUST NOT return a value higher than the actual maximum that would be accepted" — every integrator that trusts it (the `Wrapper`, 4626 routers, liquidators unwinding cUSD collateral) breaks. Round-1 identity `balanceOf(stablecoin) == unlockedSupply()` no longer holds; it becomes `balanceOf + aeraHeld == unlockedSupply()` (proven below) and only the first term is spendable. Griefing: a keeper `invest` landing before a large settled claim blocks it until a discretionary `recall`; with `x = 1 cUSD` on a 1000 cUSD reserve a full 1000-share claim reverts (`test_N2a_queuedClaimGriefedByInvest`). If Aera is paused or refuses (`test_N2c_recallRevertsWhenAeraRefuses_reserveStuck`), redemptions above the liquid remainder are dead for the duration.
**Likelihood:** Only KEEPER can trigger, but KEEPER is the automated hot role (also `report`, `extendAdmin`, market creation), `invest` has no cap relative to `unlockedSupply − redemptionQueue`, and nothing in the contract prevents investing funds already owed to settled queue positions. Cost to the keeper: zero. Cost to users: temporary freeze, wasted gas, broken integrations; escalates to the High above the moment Aera returns less.
**Exploit path (`test_N2a_maxRedeemUnchangedButRedeemRevertsAfterInvest`):** Alice deposits 1000 → `invest(600)` → `maxRedeem(alice) = maxWithdraw = 1000`, liquid 400 → `redeem(401)` reverts `ERC20InsufficientBalance(sc, 400e18, 401e18)`, `redeem(400)` ok → `maxRedeem` now 600 with liquid 0, `redeem(1)` reverts → `recall(600)` repairs.
**Proof:** the three `test_N2a_*`/`test_N2c_recall*` unit tests; stateful `N2.invariants.t.sol::N2MaxRedeemInvariants::invariant_maxRedeemIsActuallyRedeemable` (snapshot, `redeem(maxRedeem(a))`, revert) **FAILS on the first run, counterexample shrunk to 2 calls: `deposit → invest`**. The no-loss suite `N2NoLossInvariants` (256 runs × 500 = 128,000 calls, 0 reverts) proves the restated identity `liquid + aeraHeld == unlockedSupply()` and `badDebt + creditBackedSupply + unlockedSupply == totalSupply` hold exactly through deposit/redeem/requestRedeem/claim/borrow/repay/writeOff/coverBadDebt/fund/invest/recall — so the lie is purely on the liquidity side, not the accounting.
**Recommendation:** (1) Bound `invest`: `amount ≤ (liquid − queuedAssets) × maxInvestBps / 1e4` where `queuedAssets = previewRedeem(redemptionQueue())`, with `maxInvestBps` a governance parameter; never invest what a settled queue position can already claim. (2) Make the views honest: `maxRedeem = min(curve, previewWithdraw(liquidBalance))` and clamp `claimableRedeemRequest` by liquid balance (or, cheaper, override `_transferOut` in `Stablecoin` to `recall` the shortfall just-in-time when `balance < assets`; this keeps the views correct whenever Aera is live and makes an Aera refusal the only failure). Second-order of the JIT recall: every large redeem pays Aera withdraw gas, and an Aera pause turns into a redeem revert — acceptable, and strictly better than today. (3) Emit the invested amount in a view (`investedReserve()`) so integrators and the Wrapper can compute liquid capacity.
**Invariant broken:** I1 as stated in round 1; restated below.

### [LOW] `reserveVault` is fixed at `initialize`, has no setter, and the deploy config defaults it to `address(0)`
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:74` (`reserveVault = _reserveVault`), `script/config/WalletUsersConfig.sol:21` (`VM.envOr("RESERVE_VAULT", address(0))`), `test/shared/CapDeployer.sol:202`
**Impact:** With `reserveVault == 0` both `invest` and `recall` revert cleanly (`forceApprove(0)` → `ERC20InvalidSpender` on OZ ERC20; `IAeraVault(0).withdraw` fails the extcodesize check) — no silent loss (`test_N2c_investAndRecallRevertWithZeroReserveVault`). The defect is operational: an accidental mainnet deploy with the env var unset permanently disables the feature, and a deprecated/paused/migrated Aera vault can only be swapped by a UUPS upgrade. If Aera refuses `withdraw` while funds are invested, the reserve is stuck for the duration and the Medium above becomes a hard freeze (`test_N2c_recallRevertsWhenAeraRefuses_reserveStuck`).
**Likelihood:** Config/ops error or an Aera lifecycle event; no attacker. Mitigated by `_authorizeUpgrade` (ADMIN) — hence Low.
**Exploit path:** n/a (operational).
**Proof:** `N2.t.sol::test_N2c_*`.
**Recommendation:** ADMIN `setReserveVault(address)` that requires `invested == 0` (or performs a full `recall` first), and a deploy-time `require(reserveVault != 0 || !investEnabled)`. Second-order: a setter is one more admin-key lever over the reserve; pair it with the `invest` cap.
**Invariant broken:** none.

### [INFORMATIONAL] Allowance hygiene of `invest` is correct against the mocked Aera semantics; the real vault's auth gate is the unverified assumption
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:105-108`
`forceApprove(reserveVault, amount)` followed by a pull of exactly `amount`; the mock (mirroring Aera V3 `SingleDepositorVault.deposit`) reverts if any allowance remains, and `test_N2d_noLeftoverAllowanceAfterInvest` confirms allowance is 0 after each invest, including a second invest that goes non-zero → non-zero via `forceApprove`. No leftover pull right exists. Note the real Aera `deposit`/`withdraw` are owner/auth-gated: the Stablecoin proxy must be the vault's authorised depositor or every `invest` reverts (safe failure). Not demonstrable as a loss; Informational.

### [INFORMATIONAL] Public `fund` can be used by anyone to push utilization (and the borrow rate) down, at the cost of donating the full amount
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:78-81`
`fund` deposits at par and mints supply to the pot, so `utilizationRate = credit / totalSupply` falls (`test_N2e_fundByOutsiderLowersUtilization`). A borrower who funds 1000 to shave rate on a fixed borrow gives away 1000 to opted-in holders; the interest saved is a small fraction of that, so it is never profitable as an attack. The minted pot shares are fully backed (`liquid == unlockedSupply` after the call), so I1 is not affected. Sandwiching the 12 h exponential vesting is N6/N5 territory (Wrapper `totalAssets` includes `claimable`). Informational.

### [INFORMATIONAL] Public `coverBadDebt` is safe under front-running; `coverBadDebt(0)` is an event-spam no-op
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Stablecoin.sol:156-167`
Two coverers racing: the second is clamped to the remainder or reverts `NoBadDebt` — no double burn (`test_N2e_coverBadDebtFrontRunIsBenign`). `_burn(msg.sender, covered)` and `badDebt -= covered` move together so `unlockedSupply` and the reserve are unchanged (verified by the no-loss invariant, 9,846 `coverBadDebt` calls). `coverBadDebt(0)` with `badDebt > 0` burns nothing and emits `BadDebtCovered(_, 0)`; add `if (_amount == 0) revert`. Informational.

## Invariants

- **I1 broken** as stated in round 1 (`balanceOf(stablecoin) == unlockedSupply()`): false after the first `invest`.
- **I1' (restated, proven, no loss):** `IERC20(underlying).balanceOf(stablecoin) + aeraHeld == unlockedSupply()` (18-dp) through every path, and `badDebt + creditBackedSupply + unlockedSupply == totalSupply`.
- **I1'' (with loss, proven):** `unlockedSupply() − (liquid + aeraHeld) == Σ aeraLoss` and `badDebt` never moves because of it — this is the defect, not a guarantee.
- **NEW I25 (violated):** `redeem(maxRedeem(a))` never reverts; `redeem(id, claimableRedeemRequest(id, c))` never reverts. Should be added to the lead suite; currently fails for any invested state.
- **NEW I26 (proposed for the fix):** `invested ≤ (unlockedSupply − redemptionQueue) × maxInvestBps` and `invested == Σ invest − Σ recall − Σ recognisedReserveLoss`.

## Appendix: gas & style
- `invest`/`recall` build a one-element `TokenAmount[]` each call; a private helper would remove the duplication.
- `IAeraVault` exposes neither valuation nor fee views; a `value()`-style read would be needed for any permissionless loss recognition.
