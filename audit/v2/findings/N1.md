## Findings

### [MEDIUM] Wrapper first-depositor inflation is free: premium accrues to a 1-wei Wrapper and rounds later depositors to zero shares
**Location:** `contracts/cap/Wrapper.sol:35-50,62-65` (`initialize` opts in, `totalAssets`, `_deposit`); OZ `ERC4626Upgradeable._decimalsOffset() == 0`, no `DeadShares` seeding (unlike `Tranche._deposit` L178-179)
**Impact:** Any deposit `V` into the Wrapper while `totalAssets/(totalSupply+1)` is large mints `floor(V·(S+1)/(A+1))` shares. With `S = 1` and `A` inflated, `V < A/2` mints **0 shares** and the depositor's cUSD is split between the attacker's share and the virtual share; larger deposits lose up to `A/2` to rounding. The attacker needs **no donation**: the Wrapper opts in at init and, until other cUSD holders opt in, it is the *only* opted-in holder, so `fundCreditBacked` premium vests 100 % to the Wrapper regardless of how much cUSD it holds. Loss = the entire deposit for victims under `A/2`; attacker profit = `V/2`.
**Likelihood:** Launch window / any time Wrapper supply is tiny (it is deployed empty and the round-1 `stakedStablecoin` is gone, so this is *the* staking entry point). Attacker capital: 1 wei of cUSD plus being first. No privileged role. Victims: any UI/router deposit without a min-shares check (OZ `deposit` does not revert on 0 shares).
**Exploit path:** (1) Wrapper deployed; attacker `deposit(1)` → 1 share. (2) Markets charge premium (`fundCreditBacked(1000e18)`); 2 days pass; `Wrapper.totalAssets ≈ 981e18`, supply 1. (3) Victim `deposit(400e18)` → 0 shares. (4) Attacker `redeem(1)` → 690.8e18 cUSD (490.8 premium + 200 of the victim's deposit; the other 200 is locked behind the virtual share). Net: attacker +690e18 on 1 wei; victim −400e18.
**Proof:** `audit/v2/tests/scratch/N1/PoC.t.sol::test_N6b_premiumInflation_zeroCost_firstDepositor` (logs: victim shares 0; attacker out 690842604525483898843). Donation variant `test_N6_firstDepositor_inflation_costsAttacker` (2000e18 donation zeroes a 1000e18 deposit at a 500e18 cost to the attacker).
**Recommendation:** Seed the Wrapper like the other vaults — mint `DeadShares.SHARES` to `DeadShares.HOLDER` on the first deposit *and* override `_decimalsOffset()` to ≥ 6 so a rounding loss is bounded to dust even if the pot is large relative to supply; alternatively have `initialize` take a seed deposit from the deployer. Second-order: the dead-share address must stay non-opted-in (already refused by `optIn`); the offset changes `previewDeposit` rounding for integrators.
**Invariant broken:** I21 holds in cUSD-per-share terms (price only rises), which is exactly the problem — new depositors are what pay for the rise. New invariant: `Wrapper.previewDeposit(V) ≥ V·(1−ε)/pricePerShare` for any V above dust.

### [LOW] `claim` zeroes the entitlement before clamping, so any amount above `balanceOf(this)` is forfeited rather than re-banked
**Location:** `contracts/utils/PremiumVesting.sol:142-155` (`claim`) with `_settle` L274-284
**Impact:** `_settlePremium` sets `pending = 0` and `debt = owed(...)` unconditionally; the clamp then pays `min(premium, held)`. If the clamp binds, `premium − held` is lost to the claimer. The NatSpec admits entitlements "can sum past the pot" through debt rounding. On the Stablecoin the clamp also includes queued redemption shares, so a binding clamp would first pay out of the queue and make the last queued `_burn(address(this))` revert (`ERC20InsufficientBalance`).
**Likelihood:** Not reached: 16384 + 12288 fuzz calls across three vault types with a `clampShortfall` ghost recorded **0** wei of shortfall and 0 queued-burn reverts; floors in `_accrue` (`perShare`) and `_owed` dominate the ceil-like effect of `owed(p1,b) − owed(p0,b)`. Kept as Low because the code path exists, is silent, and the Stablecoin variant would DoS a queued redemption by dust; demoted from Medium per schema (no failing test).
**Exploit path:** none demonstrable.
**Proof:** `Stablecoin.invariants.t.sol::invariant_I19_I20_conservation` (`clampShortfall == 0`), `invariant_I13_potPlusQueueExact` (exact pot+queue accounting holds).
**Recommendation:** Re-bank the shortfall: `if (premium > held) { $.pending[msg.sender] += premium − held; premium = held; }`. For the Stablecoin, clamp to `balanceOf(this) − redemptionQueue()` so the pot can never spend queued shares.
**Invariant broken:** none observed; strengthens I13'.

### [LOW] Opt-in step makes tranche depositors fully slashable while earning nothing, and an un-opted tranche silently reroutes its premium
**Location:** `contracts/utils/PremiumVesting.sol:114-123` (`optIn`), `contracts/cap/market/BaseMarket.sol:404-432` (`_chargePremium`), `contracts/cap/Underwriter.sol:85` (only the Underwriter auto-opts)
**Impact:** `Tranche.deposit` does not opt in; a depositor who misses `optIn()` bears 100 % of `slash` and earns 0. If nobody in the junior tranche is opted in, `_chargePremium` skips it and its weight goes to the senior; if no tranche is opted in, the entire underwriter premium is `fundCreditBacked` to cUSD stakers while tranche capital still underwrites. The borrower pays the same; the loss is a transfer from unaware tranche holders to senior/cUSD stakers. A tranche with a single 1-wei opted-in holder receives its full weight share and that holder captures all of it (the "freeze then 1-wei opt-in" path: `test_N5_freeze_then_optIn_is_not_a_cliff` shows a 1-wei holder collecting 99.99 % of a 100e18 frozen pot in 5.5 days).
**Likelihood:** Requires only user omission; every test helper (`_fundTranche/_fundUnderwriter`) papers over it by opting in for the depositor. Cost to a "capturer": dust.
**Exploit path:** (1) Junior tranche has 1M of deposits, none opted in. (2) Attacker deposits 1 wei and opts in. (3) Every `chargePremium` sends the junior weight share to the tranche pot; `staked == 1`; attacker claims 100 % of junior premium. Profit = junior premium stream; capital = 1 wei + admission.
**Proof:** `PoC.t.sol::test_N5_freeze_then_optIn_is_not_a_cliff` (economics); the reroute branches read directly at `BaseMarket.sol:408,430`.
**Recommendation:** Opt receivers in on first deposit by default (opt-out model), or make `_chargePremium` weight by `activeSupply` and let unstaked balances' share stay in the pot; at minimum emit and document. Second-order: default opt-in re-enables the round-1 L-14 accounting on queued shares (already handled by `_update`).
**Invariant broken:** none; economic.

### [INFORMATIONAL] Freeze semantics: a zero-staked pot restarts its 12 h curve at the next opt-in; not a cliff
`_accrue` with `supply == 0` only moves `lastUpdate` (L243-246); `optIn` runs `updatePremium` *before* `staked += bal`, so the first accrual after opt-in sees `elapsed` from the opt-in, not from the freeze. Proven: `claimable == 0` immediately after opt-in, 63.2 % after 12 h (`1 − 1/e`), 99.99 % after 5.5 d. `remaining()` view reads ahead (`_vested` ignores that supply is 0) so `remaining()` under-reports while frozen — cosmetic; `vested()+remaining()` still equals `remainder`.

### [INFORMATIONAL] N3 refuted — pot and queue cannot cross-contaminate under the current rounding
Exact accounting `balanceOf(stablecoin) == Σfunded − Σpaid + donations + redemptionQueue` held over 16384 handler calls including `recognizeBadDebt`/`coverBadDebt` while queue and pot coexist; `_burn(address(this))` only ever burns queued shares; pot shares take the bad-debt haircut only at redemption like any holder. I13 restated for the Stablecoin: `balanceOf(stablecoin) ≥ redemptionQueue() + remaining()`.

### [INFORMATIONAL] N4/N12 refuted — no `_update` revert path from vesting; error selector changes for opted-in over-spend
`staked` never drifted (I18 on all three vaults). The only new revert path: an opted-in sender transferring more than its balance panics (0x11) inside `_checkpointShares` (`balance − amount`, L206) *before* `super._update` would raise `ERC20InsufficientBalance`; non-opted-in senders still get the custom error. Integrators matching on the OZ error see a Panic instead. Fix: check `amount <= balance` first or move the checkpoint after the balance check.

### [INFORMATIONAL] `rayPow` bounded; JIT confirmation; slot verified
`rayPow(1e27 − 1e27/43200, e)` for `e ≤ 10 y`: never reverts, `≤ RAY`, strictly `< RAY` for `e > 0`, monotone (256 runs each). JIT (M-3 economics, owned by G2): a same-block Wrapper depositor holding half the stake takes 31.6e18 of a 100e18 charge after 12 h (`test_N6_JIT_frontrun_of_fundCreditBacked`). ERC-7201 slot `0xcd5f59be…1c00` recomputed in-test and equals `keccak256(abi.encode(uint256(keccak256("cap.storage.PremiumVesting"))−1)) & ~0xff`; distance to every `cap.storage.*` and `openzeppelin.storage.*` namespace root > 2^64 (`test_slot_matchesERC7201`). `Wrapper` redemptions are never gated by `Stablecoin.unlockedSupply` (it pays cUSD, not underlying): `test_N6_totalAssets_neverOverstates_and_haircutDoesNotBlock` exits after a 1500e18 haircut; `totalAssets` projection never exceeded what `claim` paid.

## Invariants
- I18, I19, I20, I21 held on all vault types (counts above). I21 holds *because* of the Medium above — restate as a per-depositor fairness bound (see finding).
- I13 for the Stablecoin: `balanceOf(stablecoin) ≥ redemptionQueue() + remaining()`; exact form `== Σfunded − Σpaid + donations + queue`.
- New: `claim(x)` must be entitlement-preserving: `paid + pendingAfter == entitlementBefore` (currently false when the clamp binds).
- New: `Wrapper.previewDeposit(V) > 0` for any `V ≥ 1e6` wei (fails today after premium inflation).

## Appendix: gas & style
- cUSD `transfer` with an active pot and two opted-in parties: **129,871 gas** cold (accrue + `rayPow` + two checkpoints), 22,133 warm; `rayPow` cost is O(log elapsed) so a 10-year gap adds < 3k. Roughly 2.5× a plain ERC-20 transfer on every cUSD movement, including Wrapper deposits and market flows.
- `optIn`/`optOut` are no-ops for `address(this)`/`DeadShares.HOLDER` but emit nothing and return silently — consider reverting so a mis-wired integrator notices.
- `Stablecoin.fund(0)` succeeds and emits `Fund(0)`.
